Word Cloud Video Maker

Version 1.0.0

Turn a list of words, or any text you paste in, into a word cloud shaped like a heart, a star, or any picture you upload. Then animate it and save it as a picture, a video, or an animated GIF.

The app runs entirely in your web browser. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.
